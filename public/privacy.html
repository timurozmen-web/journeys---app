Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Journeys is a personal travel-tracking application built and used by a single individual. It is not a commercial product, and it is not available for public sign-up. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

What data is collected

  • Travel data you enter yourself: hotel stays, flights, trips, loyalty programme balances, vouchers, and promotions.
  • Confirmation emails or screenshots you choose to submit, used only to extract booking details (hotel name, dates, cost, etc).
  • Bank transaction data, if you choose to connect a bank account: transaction dates, amounts, and descriptions for accounts you personally link. This is used only to help track spend toward loyalty programme thresholds.

How data is stored

All data is stored in a private Supabase database, protected by row-level security so that only the authenticated account owner can access it. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Third-party processors

To provide its features, the app sends limited data to the following services, solely for the purpose described:

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — reads submitted confirmation emails/screenshots and promotion screenshots to extract structured booking or promotion details.
  • Enable Banking — a regulated Open Banking provider used to retrieve transaction data from accounts you explicitly connect and authorize through your bank's own consent flow.
  • Unsplash — used to source destination photography; no personal data is sent.

Bank account access

Bank connections are established only with your explicit authorization through your bank's own secure login and consent screen. Access is read-only (no payments can be initiated) and limited to account information and transaction history. You can revoke access at any time through your bank or by disconnecting within the app.

Data retention and deletion

Data is retained for as long as the account exists. Since this is a single-user personal application, deletion requests are handled directly by the account owner deleting the relevant records or the account itself.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the account owner via the contact details associated with this application's registration.
